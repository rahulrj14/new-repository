Lock In privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · App: Lock In (Android, app.lockin.android)

Lock In collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no internet access. Everything stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your blob, skin and nickname (chosen from a fixed list), your sessions, aura, streaks, quests, skins and stickers. These are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Cloud backup is switched off, so they are not copied to Google Drive. A direct phone-to-phone transfer, for example when you set up a new phone, can move them.

Squads (friends in the same room)

When you lock in with friends, nearby phones talk to each other over Bluetooth. The messages contain only preset IDs and numbers: which blob, nickname and skin you picked from the app's fixed lists, your session status (for example "locked" or "cracked") and counters (for example minutes and cracks). There is no chat, no free text, no photos and no contact details.

  • Joining needs the host's QR code, scanned in person, and the host's approval.
  • Messages are encrypted. Only phones that scanned that session's code can read them.
  • These messages go directly between phones. They never reach us or any server.

Permissions and why

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShows the "Locked in" notification and timer while a session you started is running.
Foreground serviceKeeps your session running while your phone is locked, so the app can notice an unlock.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)Finds and connects to friends' phones in the same room for squads. Not used for location.
Location (Android 8–11 only)Those Android versions only allow Bluetooth scanning if this permission is granted. Lock In asks only when you start or join a squad, and never reads, stores or sends your location. Android 12 and later don't need it.
CameraScans a friend's join code. Images are processed in memory and never saved or sent.
Wake lockKeeps squad updates flowing while phones are locked.

Sharing

If you tap share on a card or sticker, Lock In makes an image on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you choose.

Feedback

The "send feedback" link opens a web form in your browser. Anything you type there goes to that form, not through the app. Please don't include personal details you don't want to share.

Crash reports

Lock In includes no crash-reporting or analytics code. Google Play may show us anonymous crash and "app not responding" statistics that Android collects under your own device settings.

Teens

Lock In is for ages 13 and up. It follows the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: private by default, no profiling, no ads, no contact with strangers, and no nudges designed to keep you on your phone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date before the change ships.
